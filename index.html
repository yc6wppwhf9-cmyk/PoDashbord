How upload works

Upload the Purchase_Order_Report_New export as it comes from the ERP. Title rows and the totals row are skipped automatically. Each upload is a full snapshot, so it replaces the register. Your tracked PO numbers stay, and any status or pending change on a tracked PO is added to its history.
